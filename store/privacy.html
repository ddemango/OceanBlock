D's Cannon Blast Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

D's Cannon Blast ("the app") is a single-player game. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it has no user accounts.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (current level, stars, coins, hearts, daily-reward streak and settings such as sound and vibration) is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the app.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Third parties

The app does not send data to third parties. If you install the app through Apple's TestFlight or the App Store, Apple's own privacy policy applies to those services.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to collect information (for example, if ads or in-app purchases are added), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
