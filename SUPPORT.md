# D's Cannon Blast — Support

Thanks for playing **D's Cannon Blast**!

## How to play
- **Hold your finger** on the screen to fire the cannon; drag to aim.
- **Sink every piece of cargo** before you run out of cannonballs.
- Fill the **bomb meter** to fire a giant bomb.
- Use **power-ups** (Mega Blast, Slow-Mo, Laser Aim) once per level.
- Collect a free **Daily Reward**, a **Lucky Spin** and a new **Daily Challenge** every day.

## Common questions
**My progress disappeared.** Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

**How do I get more hearts?** Hearts refill one every 15 minutes, or tap *Refill* for coins.

**The Leaderboard button doesn't work.** Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings → Game Center.
