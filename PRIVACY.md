# D's Cannon Blast — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 28, 2026_

D's Cannon Blast ("the app") is a single-player game. **The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.**

## What the app stores
- Your game progress (level, coins, stars, unlocked items and settings) is saved **only on your device**. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Game Center
- If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your Daily Challenge score is sent to **Apple's Game Center** leaderboard so it can be compared with other players. This is handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not receive your Game Center details.

## What the app does not do
- No accounts or sign-up
- No advertising and no ad tracking
- No analytics or third-party tracking tools
- No in-app purchases
- No collection of location, contacts, photos or any other personal data

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
